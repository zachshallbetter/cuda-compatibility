# Support

CUDA Compatibility is maintained by one person. Support is best-effort, with no SLA.

- **Questions and bugs:** open a [GitHub issue](https://github.com/zachshallbetter/cuda-compatibility/issues/new/choose). To report how a published example behaved on your hardware, use the reproduction report form.
- **Security reports:** follow [SECURITY.md](SECURITY.md). Do not open a public issue.
- **Versioning:** the project is released as `cuda-legacy-compat` and tagged `vX.Y.Z`. It is experimental and pre-1.0; only the latest release receives fixes.
